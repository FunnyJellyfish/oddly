Origin

It started with a friend.

Stephen Stubborn has a friend named Vincenzo. Vincenzo is Italian. Vincenzo also has what he describes, with his permission and in his words, as "self-lubricating hair." He wakes up, and it is already shining. He has never purchased a product for it. He does not know what a product would be for.

Stephen Stubborn does not have that privilege. Stephen Stubborn wakes up, and his hair is in the same condition as Stephen Stubborn: determined, but dry.

So he did what he always does. He looked at Vincenzo's hair and said, "This can be improved." Vincenzo said it could not. Stephen agreed, and clarified that he meant his own hair. The two of them spent a weekend at Vincenzo's kitchen table designing the Oddly Hair Lubricator. Vincenzo supplied the oil, the technique, and the hair to copy. Stephen supplied the robotic lice.

"Vincenzo's hair does this by itself. Mine needs 1,200 robots. That is not a disadvantage. That is a supply chain."— Stephen Stubborn, pronounced "Stefen Stu-Born"

How it works

Four steps. One swarm.

Each Oddly Hair Lubricator ships with 1,200 robotic lice. Legally, they are not lice. Internally, we call them Follicle Associates. They call each other nothing. They are focused.

01

Deploy

Hold the Hive Comb to your head and press the button. The swarm disembarks in an orderly fashion. Some of them wave.

02

Swarm

The lice map every strand. A full head is surveyed in under a minute. Ponytails are surveyed twice, for fairness.

03

Lubricate

Each louse carries a micro-reservoir of oil and works one strand from root to tip. Then the next strand. Then yours again, to be sure.

04

Return

At the selected level, the swarm marches back into the Hive Comb to recharge overnight. Most of them. The rest are considered loyal.

Questions

Are they lice?

They are robotic lice. Robotic lice are not lice, in the same way a robotic dog is not a dog. Ours are better than a robotic dog. They oil things.

Will they leave my head?

They return to the Hive Comb at the end of every session. A small number may remain, out of commitment. We consider this a feature and refer to them as your resident team.

Can my friends catch them?

Only if your friends ask nicely. Follicle Associates do not transfer between heads without a valid Oddly account.

What does the Vincenzo setting do?

It matches Vincenzo. We measured him on a Tuesday. He was not informed that Tuesday was the measurement. His hair did not change, because it never does.

Is it safe?

Certification is pending. It has been pending for some time. This is fine.
