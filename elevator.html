What We Removed

Three Things Were Holding Elevators Back.

Doors. Safety systems. And what our CEO refers to internally as "useless junk." We took all three out. The result is an elevator that is, by weight, mostly elevator.

01

No Doors

Doors open. Doors close. Doors wait for you to get out of the way. The Oddly Elevator has no doors, which means it never has to wait for anything, including you.

02

No Full Stops

Without doors, there's no reason to fully stop at a floor. The ODL-1 slows to a brisk pace as it passes your floor. We call this "arriving."

03

No Useless Junk

Emergency brakes, governors, buffer springs, and the little phone in the wall — all removed. Every one of those parts was just adding weight the elevator had to move.
