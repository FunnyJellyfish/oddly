From the leadership

Choose your oTag.

Legal. oTag is always listening, always watching and always scanning. This is how it knows it is under the bush. Descriptive Finding™ describes the location of the oTag relative to nearby objects; it does not say which bush. oTag in Quiet Mode disables the camera, LiDAR, speakers, microphones and oSiri, extending battery life to one year, at which point it is a tag.

Unwanted Tracking Protection: an oTag that travels with someone who does not own it will announce, at full volume and at regular intervals, "Hello. I am an oTag. I am in your bag. I belong to someone else." Oddly, Inc. considers this a safety feature and a conversation starter.

Facial recognition accuracy measured on the Stephens. Pet recognition does not cover fish, which do not leave the house. Track Anything tracks anything in frame and nothing out of frame.
