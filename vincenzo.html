Stubborn's Friends · Co-inventor

Vincenzo.

Italian · self-lubricating · not an employee

Stephen Stubborn's friend, and co-inventor of the Oddly Hair Lubricator. Vincenzo wakes up and his hair is already shining. He has never bought a product for it. He does not know what a product would be for. Stephen Stubborn looked at that and said: this can be improved. Vincenzo said it could not. They were both right.

Frequently asked questions

Asked. Answered. Shining.

Does he really use nothing?

Nothing. He has asked us not to look into it. We looked into it. There is nothing. The Hair Lubricator's oil is "Vincenzo's supplier," which turned out to be a grocery store he walks past.

Does he use the Oddly Hair Lubricator?

No. He was given the first unit. He gave it to his cousin, who now uses the Vincenzo setting and reports that it is "close."

Is the "Vincenzo" setting named after him?

Yes. It is the top setting. It takes 11 minutes and 1,200 robots to reach what he reaches by getting up.

Does he work at Oddly, Inc.?

No. He is a friend. Stephen Stubborn has offered him the title "Chief Shine Officer" several times. He has said "no, grazie" several times. Both men consider the matter open.

Is he related to Stephen Stupid?

No. Stephen Stupid is not related to anyone at Oddly, Inc., including the people he is not related to.

Is he at the business dinner photo?

Yes. Far right, laughing. His hair is doing that by itself. See The Journey of Oddly.
