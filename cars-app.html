Oddly Roadster exclusive

Rocket Controls

The Oddly Roadster is a seven-seat minivan with two actual rockets. Not cold-gas thrusters. Rockets. Now you can light them from the school pick-up line.

  • Three-switch arming sequence with hazard-striped safety covers
  • Thrust modes: School Run, Commute, Ludicrous, and Orbital (FAA waiver pending)
  • Hold-to-ignite. Abort button submits a request.
  • Live telemetry: speed, altitude, propellant, seatbelt status (advisory)
  • Passenger Acknowledgement from all 7 seats, including the empty ones
BETA SINCE AUGUST
Powered by Oddly Autonomous™

Oddly Autonomous Summon

Press Summon and your car leaves its parking space and drives to a location. The location is chosen by the car. You set your pin so the car knows what it is not doing.

  • Works from any distance, in any direction
  • ETA: Indeterminate. Updated constantly.
  • Scheduled duck pond stops so the car can think
  • Cancel button submits a request. The car considers it.
  • Arrival location is final
A typical summon

Six minutes. Excellent minutes.

0:00

You press Summon. The car acknowledges. The car does not say yes.

0:40

Car is heading away from you. This is part of the route.

2:15

Car stops at a duck pond to think. Engineers say this is normal.

4:50

You press Cancel. Cancellation request submitted.

6:02

Your car has arrived. It is 3 blocks from you. Summon complete.
