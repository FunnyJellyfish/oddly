Questions

Is it safe to wear a capacitor bank?

The lining is grounded and the elements are sealed. The energy goes where it is told. It has always gone where it is told. Certification is pending to confirm this.

Why does my hair stand up?

The jacket is charged. So, a little, are you. It settles after discharge. We consider it a charge indicator you can see from across the street.

Can I wash it?

After discharge, yes, on cold. Never wash a charged oJacket. The washing machine will become warm very quickly, and then it will become a different appliance.

What happens if I press Discharge twice?

Nothing. The capacitors are empty. The jacket will vibrate once, politely, to remind you that you already did.

Can I fly with it?

Please discharge it at the gate. Several airports have asked us to say this.
