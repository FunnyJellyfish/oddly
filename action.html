How it works

Unbox. Step back.

01

Unbox

Open the box. The figures climb out. Please hold the flap. They appreciate it.

02

Wake

Each figure stretches, looks around, and picks a spot. The spot is usually the edge of a shelf. They like the view.

03

Move

Patrols, poses, dance-offs and foam battles, all on their own. Suggest a mode in the app. They take suggestions seriously, mostly.

04

Bedtime

At 8 PM they march back into the box in a single file and close the lid from inside. Every night. Without being asked.

Questions

Do they move on their own?

Yes. That is the entire product. You can also move them yourself. They will thank you, wait a moment, and move back.

Are the battles safe?

Battles use foam darts and choreographed slow-motion. Nobody is ever hit. The losing figure lies down dramatically and then gets up for a group photo.

Will they leave the room?

On Patrol, they will check the hallway and come back. They always come back. They have a curfew.

What if I want them to stand still?

Turn on Freeze When Watched in the app. They will hold perfectly still whenever anyone is looking, which, they would like you to know, is much harder than moving.

Are they safe?

Certification is pending. It has been pending for some time. This is fine.
