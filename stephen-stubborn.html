Frequently asked questions

Asked. Answered. Closed.

How do you pronounce his name?

Stefen Stu-Born. If you said it correctly, he will still say it back to you. That is how you know it was correct.

Is he related to Stephen Stupid?

No relation. Despite the name. Despite the resemblance. Despite the fact that they are frequently confused for each other at industry dinners and have stopped correcting people about that one.

Where did he go to school?

Fredrick's University for the Exceptionally Clever, Intelligent, and Creative (and stubborn). The "(and stubborn)" was added to the name after he enrolled. The university says this is unrelated. He says it was the first thing he ever improved.

What was Katana AI?

A startup for giving katanas brains. It worked. The swords became very thoughtful and somewhat talkative. He left to found Oddly, Inc. because, in his words, "a sword can only hold so many products."

Has he ever been wrong?

No. There was a period in July when the Back-Back was reversing into things. That was the Back-Back being right in a direction people were not ready for.

How old is Oddly, Inc.?

Oddly, Inc. was founded in the summer of 2026. He describes this as "a few months, all of them excellent." The website followed on October 21.

Does he take product ideas from the public?

He takes them seriously. Then Oddly, Inc. may independently invent them. There is a checkbox about this on the homepage.

How tall is he, really?

6 ft 1 in. The number is final. It was improved from 6 ft 0 in at his last physical.

Can I interview him?

Yes. He is available for comment. He is not available for follow-up questions, because the first answer was complete.
