Why Oddly Cars

Every part of it is a feature.

01

Two of everything

Two fronts or two backs. Never one of each. One of each is what everyone else does, and we did not want to be seen copying.

02

No reverse gear

Reverse has been removed. It was not needed. The car is already facing the direction you are going. Both of them.

03

Intuitive routing

Oddly Autonomous™ does not follow maps. Maps were made by other people. It follows a feeling, and the feeling is ours.

04

Premium confusion

Pedestrians cannot tell which way an Oddly is facing. Neither can the Oddly. This keeps everyone alert. We call it a safety system.

05

Ownership, deferred

Our CFO does not believe in depreciation. He believes in deferred value. Your car is worth more every year, on paper, on our paper.
