What's New in oOS 2.0
• Oddly Brain: Messages, calls, Siri and oSafari now work for everyone, no server required. Siri can set timers, take notes, play music and look things up.
• Every setting does something. Airplane Mode, volume, notifications, keyboard, Face Unlock, privacy and more now really work, and are remembered.
• oStore Companion Apps for Oddly Cars, Human 1, the Plane Pressurizer, NukeMow, the Pool Cleaner and the Hair Lubricator.
• Widgets: long-press the Home Screen to add Calendar, Notes, Now Playing and Timer.
• oMusic now has both songs. oArcade adds Snake and Breakout.
• Real Dark Mode, a working Stopwatch and Timer, and 24-hour time.
• The 5 AM alarm is unchanged. It is a wellness feature.
