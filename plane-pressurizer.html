How it works

Simple. We pressurize the outside of your ear.

Ears pop because the air inside your head disagrees with the air inside the plane. The Pressurizer does not settle the argument. It surrounds your ear with a third opinion.

01 / SEAL

Attach the chambers.

Two 19-inch domes clamp over each ear and seal against your skull with a medical-grade gasket. And a strap.

02 / READ

Measure your head.

Sensors estimate your middle-ear pressure. The estimate is confident.

03 / MATCH

Push or pull.

Climbing? The chamber pressurizes. Descending? It depressurizes. Your eardrum feels equal pressure on both sides. It feels nothing else.

04 / UN-POP

Ears un-popped.

The pop never happens. We call this un-popping. We have filed for the word.

One-tap Un-Pop

The app reads the cabin, estimates your head, and moves both chambers to the correct pressure. Estimated time: two seconds. Estimated estimate: confident.

Independent Ear Control

Tap LEFT or RIGHT to unlink them. Run each ear at its own pressure. Your ears are individuals. We respect that.

Vent

Releases chamber pressure instantly. Air exits through the side ports, toward Seats A and C. This is called sharing.

Neighbor Notifications

When an adjacent passenger submits a complaint, oPressure lets you know right away. It also declines the complaint for you.
