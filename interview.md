# Claire Cookoo Interviews Stephen Stubborn About the oPhone 1

**[Scene: Tech stage. Giant ODDLY logo glows behind two chairs. The oPhone 1 rotates suspiciously like an iPhone.]**

**Claire Cookoo:**  
Hello everyone! Today I’m joined by *Steven Stub-burn*, CEO of Oddly—

**Stephen Stubborn:**  
Stefen Stu born.

**Claire:**  
Right. Steven—

**Stephen:**  
Stefen Stu born.

---

**Claire:**  
So tell us about the new oPhone 1.

**Stephen:**  
Simple. We looked at smartphones and said:

*“This can be improved.”*

So we made perfection.

---

**Claire:**  
It looks exactly like an iPhone.

**Stephen:**  
Coincidence.

Rectangles are universal.

---

**Claire:**  
You’re advertising *Oddly Liquid Glass.*

Now, I recently tried Apple’s Liquid Glass and honestly…

Beautiful transparency. Smooth animations. Elegant design.

Really impressive.

**Stephen:**  
Yes.

And ours is better.

---

**Claire:**  
Let’s compare.

**[Apple demo appears.]**

**Claire:**  
Wow.

That looks gorgeous.

Very polished.

**Stephen:**  
Now ours.

**[Screen switches. Entire phone begins wobbling violently.]**

**Claire:**

**Stephen:**  
Fluid dynamics.

**Claire:**  
The text is blurry.

**Stephen:**  
Advanced transparency.

**Claire:**  
The wallpaper is melting.

**Stephen:**  
Premium animation.

**Claire:**  
I definitely prefer Apple’s.

**Stephen:**  
That is unfortunate.

---

**Claire:**  
This honestly looks like you copied Apple’s Liquid Glass and dropped it in a washing machine.

**Stephen:**  
We did not copy.

We independently invented glass.

---

**Claire:**  
Battery test.

Apple lasted all day.

Your phone lasted 17 minutes.

**Stephen:**  
Seventeen excellent minutes.

---

**Claire:**  
Final question.

Why buy an oPhone instead of an iPhone?

**Stephen:**  
Because Apple asks:

*“What do customers want?”*

We ask:

*“What is almost legally distinct?”*

And then—

we innovate.

---

**Claire:**  
So basically…

copy Apple badly?

**Stephen:**  
No.

We copy efficiently.

---

**Claire:**  
Well thank you, Steven Stu—

**Stephen:**  
Stefen Stu born.

**Claire:**  
Anyway…

The new oPhone 1.

Or…

just buy an iPhone.

**Stephen:**  
No.

Buy oPhone.

We have…

*(dramatic pause)*

English.

Oddly.
