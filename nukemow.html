How it works

Four steps. One mow.

NukeMow replaces every mower you will ever own with a single, final mowing event. Setup takes four minutes. Mowing takes less.

01

Measure

A sensor in your lawn measures the grass daily. At four inches, it decides that it is time. You are notified. The grass is not.

02

Locate

The Oddly app finds the nearest bunker. Oddly, Inc. operates a bunker near every lawn. We are not able to say where. Neither is the app.

03

Launch

NukeMow launches. Flight time is under a minute. The app plays a short, calming chime that is louder than you would expect.

04

Mowed

Your grass is now 0.000 inches. It will stay that way. Your lawn is now glass, which does not need mowing either.
