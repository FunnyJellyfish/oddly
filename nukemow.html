How it works

Four steps. One mow.

NukeMow replaces every mower you will ever own with a single, final mowing event. Setup takes four minutes. Mowing takes less.

01

Measure

A sensor in your lawn measures the grass daily. At four inches, it decides that it is time. You are notified. The grass is not.

02

Locate

The Oddly app finds the nearest bunker. Oddly, Inc. operates a bunker near every lawn. We are not able to say where. Neither is the app.

03

Launch

NukeMow launches. Flight time is under a minute. The app plays a short, calming chime that is louder than you would expect.

04

Mowed

Your grass is now 0.000 inches. It will stay that way. Your lawn is now glass, which does not need mowing either.

Questions

Where should I be when it mows?

Elsewhere. The app will suggest a nice restaurant in another state. Reservations are recommended, because everyone in your ZIP code will also be going.

Will it mow my neighbor's lawn?

Yes. Neighboring lawns are mowed at no extra charge. Your neighbors may not have asked for this. That is why it is free.

Why would I need more than one launch?

You would not. The grass does not come back. Additional launches are for peace of mind, and for the lawns of people who have upset you.

What about my flower beds?

Your flower beds will be mowed too. Everything will be mowed. That is what we mean by premium edging.

Is NukeMow legal?

Legal review is pending. It has been pending for some time. This is fine.

Companion app

Your lawn, in your pocket. Briefly.

The NukeMow app watches your grass, finds your nearest bunker, and lets you launch with one deliberate swipe. It is connected to the lawn above. Try it.
