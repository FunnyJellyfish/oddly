Questions

Will it burn my toast?

No. Burning takes time. The NOW Toaster does not give the toast any.

Why are my lights flickering?

You pressed NOW. For three milliseconds the toaster needed the electricity more than your lights did. The lights agree.

Why keep the Start button?

Some customers enjoy waiting. We do not understand them. We respect them. Start toasts in 2:30, exactly like a normal toaster, which is what it is.

What is the "Sun" setting?

The same 5,505 °C, held for one additional millisecond. The toast comes out golden on the outside and, according to our testers, "warm in a way that felt personal."

Can I touch the toaster after NOW?

Yes. The outside never exceeds 38 °C. The heat goes into the toast and then, apparently, somewhere else. We are looking into where.

Why does the NOW Kettle only whistle once?

The whistle is for telling you the water is ready. The water is ready 0.4 seconds after you press NOW. You are still there. You do not need to be told twice.

Is it safe?

Certification is pending. It has been pending for 0.003 seconds, which, in NOW terms, is a long time.
